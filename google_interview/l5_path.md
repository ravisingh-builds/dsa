## Weekly load (~10–12 hours)

1. **DSA** — 1–2 locks + 1 recode from blank (45-min clock).  
2. **HLD** — one 45-min sketch (`hld_topics/`).  
3. **Sunday** — one STAR (leadership, not “I closed a ticket”).  
4. **Python** — only what’s blocking the current DSA.

Do **not** drop (1) to binge design videos.
