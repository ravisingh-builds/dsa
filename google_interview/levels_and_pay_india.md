# Levels and pay (India)

## What the levels mean (SWE)

| Level | Title (approx.) | What the bar is |
|-------|-----------------|-----------------|
| L3 | Entry / early SWE | Coding + growth. Closer to campus / early career. |
| **L4** | SWE III (mid) | **Coding is the main gate.** A full system-design round is **usually skipped** in India (not a promise of 0% design). Googliness = teamwork / ownership, not “I lead orgs.” |
| L5 | Senior SWE | Coding **plus** a real **system design** round plus stronger leadership stories. |

**Prep target: L5** (noted 27 Sep 2026). Eleven years in RPA + ₹40 L CTC does **not** by itself stamp L5. Recruiter math is “can they do senior SWE.” Ask L5; a downlevel to L4 is still Google.

Downlevel L4 → L3 (or reject) happens when coding is inconsistent. Asking for L5 on the first loop is how packets die.

## India total compensation (public, not an offer)

Self-reported **Levels.fyi** (Bengaluru, ~Aug 2026) — **TC = base + stock/year + bonus**, not take-home:

| Level | Median TC (about) |
|-------|-------------------|
| L4 | **~₹73 L** (₹7.35M) — base ~₹42 L + stock/year ~₹27 L + bonus ~₹4 L |
| L5 | **~₹1.12 Cr** (₹11.2M) |

Other writeups: L4 often **₹65–85 L** TC; L5 **₹90 L–1.5 Cr**. Stock is **GSUs over 4 years**. “2× ₹40 L” is roughly L4 TC (**~1.8×**), and part is stock, not double cash in month one.

Use ₹40 L only to push a **low L4 base**, not to demand L5.

## US vs India

US medians (same site, 2026): L4 ~**$290K** TC, L5 ~**$400–430K** TC. Different tax, rent, and **visa**. Not “US band from an Indian apartment.”

## Remote / onsite

Google SWE is **office-first**. Fully remote India on a **US** band is uncommon.

Realistic: **India office** (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, …) or **relocate** if they sponsor. “Remote preferred, onsite OK (India or abroad if pay is right)” is a recruiter sentence, not a plan to skip the office.

Check a live packet on [Levels.fyi Google Bengaluru SWE](https://www.levels.fyi/companies/google/salaries/software-engineer/locations/greater-bengaluru) before treating any number as an offer.
