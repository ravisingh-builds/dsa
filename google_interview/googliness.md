# Googliness (the behavioral round)

**Not coding.** One ~45 min conversation. They ask “Tell me about a time…” and sometimes “What would you do if…”.

They look for: you work with other people, you own a mess, you learn from a miss, you don’t need a hero story, you don’t trash old teams. Google names ideas like **ambiguity**, **feedback**, **user/customer**, **doing the right thing**, **team**.

**L4:** teamwork + initiative. **L5 (your target):** that plus **leading without a title**, mentoring, a hard call, a miss you owned.

Prep is **stories**, not a course. Write STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for: a conflict, unclear requirements, you helped someone, something failed. Half a page each, over the months — not week 1 grind.

This is **not** LLD or HLD.
